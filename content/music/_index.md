I am a composer and pianist interested in music that carries memory, playfulness, and internalized emotion into sound.

## Selected works

- *Rondeau* (2025), for violoncello and piano
- *in one breath* (2024/25), for piano alone
- *Wanna Play All Day!!* (2024/25), for clarinet, viola, and piano
- *Divertimento!!* (2025), for flute, clarinet, percussion, piano, viola, and contrabass
- *George!!* (2019/25), for large orchestra
